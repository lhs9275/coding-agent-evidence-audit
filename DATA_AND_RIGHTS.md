# Data scope and rights

## Included

First-party audit programs, focused tests, structured adjudication records, selected method notes, and the manuscript source retained in the 25 August 2026 backup. `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` identifies the exact exported original bytes. Release documentation and the Korean technical report are new archival material dated 26 October 2026.

## Excluded

The private backup's Git history, internal AI discussions and reviews, credentials, environment caches, full-text publication caches, downloaded external repositories, benchmark payloads, and large upstream run archives are not redistributed. This is a bounded export, not the full backup.

Structured records may contain source locators and historical local-path labels. Such labels describe the archived observation context; they are not working paths promised by this release. Numeric adjudications and protocol digests have not been silently rewritten to modernize those labels.
